## Android and Wear OS

`wear/` and `android/` use the standard Gradle wrapper (Gradle, Apache-2.0) and pull their
dependencies from Google's Maven repository and Maven Central at build time. The declared set is in
`wear/app/build.gradle.kts` and `android/app/build.gradle.kts`: AndroidX Activity Compose, Compose
BOM, Core KTX, Material3, Wear Compose, Wear Tiles, Protolayout, Guava, DataStore, OkHttp, KotlinX
Serialization and Coroutines, and JUnit. Their licences travel with those artifacts.

## Data and artwork

- Weather data comes from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (CC BY 4.0). The weather fast path
  calls the forecast endpoint, and its keyless geocoder when a place is named, then stores nothing.
- The mascot frames in `wear/app/src/main/assets/` are generated by the bundled `tools/facesmith`;
  the Nous looks are this project's artwork.
- The Inter font is not bundled. The pane uses the host's interface font.
- `protocol/PROTOCOL.md` and `docs/` are original to this project.
